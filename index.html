What Can You Expect From Us?

Region-Based Co-operation

Our team consists of members in various regions countrywide in order to better identify ways for our programme to engage efficiently with different parts of the country.

Our usage of specialized teaching kits, schooling equipment and resources allows us to provide secondary school students with both proper and relevant literacy training courses.
