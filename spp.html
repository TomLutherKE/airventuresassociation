School Pilot Programmes

Through our extensive network of faculty and administrative heads in the secondary education sector willing to co-operate with us, our team has the ability to efficiently provide staff and community trainers where needed.

By investing in and expanding on the aviation education sector, we hope to encourage the exploration of this topic of education initially to schools that possess the infrastructure required to house majority of the resources that would be used regionally.

What Is Our Vision?

To enable schools in remote, hard-to-reach areas to access proper education by the provision of schooling equipment such as mobile libraries and the repurposing of the abandoned and unused sectors of these schools.
