About Us!

We are a dedicated team of visionaries in the aviation education sector.

Since our inception, we have been working tirelessly in the creation and implementation of initiatives that would further enhance on the aspect of aviation education countrywide.

Through strategic regional bases, we have enjoyed efficient co-operation with both government officials and local leaders in ensuring our goals are met ass quickly as possible.

Our logistics and diplomatic capabilities allow us to efficiently and effectively acquire both the infrastructure and resources needed to carry out our education initiatives.

At Air Ventures Association Limited, we are committed to pursuing our vision with the utmost tenacity. Contact us if you wish to either join us or help us, should we share the same goals as you do.
